Motion · rules

1 — Colors animate. Nothing moves.

transition-colors on every hoverable element. 150 ms. No duration-* override. No translate, no scale, no rotate.

Hover any element — only colour changes. Nothing shifts position.

2 — Fade for presence.

Tooltips and dropdowns use transition-opacity with invisible/visible + opacity-0/100. 150 ms. No translate-on-enter.

3 — Instant for commits.

Form state, loading states, and route changes are instant. The one permitted animation is animate-spin — only when the wait is genuinely long (Claude plan generation: 5–15 s). A 200 ms spinner is worse than no spinner.
