Voice · specimens

  • Person. Second-person for the user ("your group", "you'll appear"); first-person plural for the product ("we sent a link", "we never post on your behalf").
  • Case. Sentence case everywhere — buttons, titles, menu items. No Title Case, no ALL CAPS except eyebrow labels (ACCOUNT, STRAVA).
  • Numbers. Always numeric: 10 pts, 5 km, 45 min, 3 days to go.
  • Punctuation. No emoji. Em-dash (—) as separator; middle-dot (·) for inline metadata. No exclamation marks except "say hello!"
  • Tone. Direct and dry — "thoughtful product person", not marketing team. Short sentences. No padding, no softening, no hype.
